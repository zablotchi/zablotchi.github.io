I'm a computer scientist working on distributed computing: consensus protocols, blockchains, and fast data-center systems. From 2023 to 2026 I was a Senior Research Scientist at Mysten Labs, the company behind the Sui blockchain. Before that, I was a postdoc in Nir Shavit's group at MIT CSAIL, and I did my PhD at EPFL with Rachid Guerraoui; my dissertation received an Honorable Mention for the EuroSys Roger Needham PhD Award.
